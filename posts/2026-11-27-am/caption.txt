You spent an hour getting the shot right, posted it, and Instagram turned it into soup. Been there 😩

Good news: most of the time it's not your phone. It's a few settings and habits. Here are 5 free fixes:

1. Turn on "Upload at highest quality". Profile > menu > Data usage and media quality. Many of us have never opened that page.
2. Switch Data saver off before you post. Saving bundles is smart for scrolling, not for uploading. Wait for good Wi-Fi or strong 4G, not that patchy signal on the moto from Kimironko to town.
3. Film with your phone's camera app, not the camera inside Instagram, then upload from your gallery.
4. Edit once. Finish in CapCut, Edits or InShot, then post without adding filters or trimming again inside Instagram. Every re-save squeezes the video again.
5. Export at the size Instagram wants: vertical 9:16, 1080 x 1920, 30fps. 4K just gets shrunk.
