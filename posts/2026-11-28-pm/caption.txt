You finally DM'd your dream podcast guest... and they left you on "seen" 🫠

Don't take it personal. Most of the time the message was just too long, too vague, or too much work to answer. Here's how to pitch so people actually say yes, even when your show is still small:

1. Do your homework. Mention one specific thing they said or made recently. "Big fan!" alone won't do it.
2. Keep it to 5 lines. Who you are, your show in one line, why them, the topic, the ask. If they have to scroll, they'll skip.
3. Make it easy. Give two or three date options, how long it takes, where you're recording, and if it's audio or video. Nobody wants 20 back-and-forth messages.
4. Tell them what's in it for them. Drop your best episode, say who listens, and promise short clips they can post on their own page.
5. Follow up once. Give it about a week, send one polite nudge. Still quiet? Say thanks and move on. No spamming people at 11pm 😅
