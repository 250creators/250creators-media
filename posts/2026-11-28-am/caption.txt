Be honest: when was the last time you watched a Reel with the sound ON? 🔇

On the moto, on the bus to Nyabugogo, in the office pretending to work... most of us scroll on mute. So if your words only live in the audio, a lot of people never hear your point. They just swipe.

4 quick fixes:

1. Let the app type it, then you fix it. Use the captions sticker in Instagram, or the caption generator in Edits or CapCut. Then read every line, because auto captions love to butcher names, slang and Kinyarwanda words.
2. Make it readable in one second. Max 2 lines, big bold font, light text on a dark box (or the reverse). Keep it away from the bottom and right edge where the buttons and caption sit.
3. Put your hook on screen before you even speak. One short line on frame one tells a muted viewer why they should stay.
4. Speak Kinyinglish? Caption on purpose. Mixing is how we talk, no stress. Just pick the caption language for who you want to reach. English captions on Kinyarwanda talk can bring in the diaspora too.
